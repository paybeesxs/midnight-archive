ARCHIVE CLOSED

I stopped explaining myself to people who only listened when the story benefited them.


Some people collect secrets like trophies, then act surprised when the truth remembers them.


I don't need revenge. I don't need an apology. And I certainly don't need you to understand.


You already showed me enough.


The strange thing about betrayal is that it rarely comes from a stranger.


Sometimes, the person holding the key is the same person who locks the door.


I remember everything. I simply stopped talking about it.
